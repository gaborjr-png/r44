The company

From family business to trusted supplier

We have been in metal cutting for more than 29 years. We started in a 200 m² workshop in Verpelét; today we manufacture in 3 shifts with around 50 employees on our own 27,000 m² site.

Our competitive advantage is that we cover the entire manufacturing process for our partners: material sourcing, production, inspection and delivery in one hand. This reduces logistics costs and simplifies the supply chain.

Quality

Certified systems, in-house metrology

We operate under ISO 9001 quality management and ISO 14001 environmental management systems. In-process and final inspection take place in our own measuring room, and processes are supported by an integrated SAP ERP system.
