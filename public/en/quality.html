Quality

Quality assurance and environmental responsibility

To meet the growing requirements of domestic and international markets, we place particular emphasis on quality assurance.

ISO 9001

Quality management system

Documented processes from quotation to delivery: contract review, production planning, inspection, control of non-conforming product and continuous improvement.

ISO 14001

Environmental management system

Controlled, responsible handling of swarf, coolants and energy consumption – with a 48.96 kWp solar PV system and solar thermal collectors.

QA

In-house measuring room

First-sample, in-process and final inspection measurements are carried out in our own measuring room, with inspection reports on request.

SAP

Integrated ERP

SAP integrated ERP and production management to track orders, stock and production lots.
