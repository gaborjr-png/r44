Developments and funded projects

We continuously develop our machine park, production floor and production management systems to meet the requirements of the domestic and international supplier market.

2020 Irinyi Plan

Development of supplier activities

The aim of the project was to develop the company’s supplier activities. It included the purchase of two STAR SB-20R type G CNC automatic lathes and the introduction of an efficiency-boosting production management system. Our employees took part in training on the new production management system.

2016 EU funding

New 1,200 m² production hall

With EU funding, a modern 1,200 m² production hall was completed at our Verpelét site to expand series production capacity.
