Capabilities

CNC machining in medium and large series

High-precision turned and milled components in aluminium, brass and engineering plastics, plus machining of aluminium and brass castings.

CNC turning CNC milling Casting machining Series production Materials Process

Technology 01

CNC turning

Complete machining of rotational parts from bar stock on CNC automatic and Swiss-type lathes.

  • INDEX GS30 / GS42, STAR SR, SA, SB and SR-32J, Hanwha and Nakamura-Tome machines
  • Automatic bar feeding (FMB, LNS, MBL, IEMCA) for long, supervised run times
  • Small, complex precision parts on Swiss-type lathes
  • Chucking work on Mori Seiki and SZIM lathes

31 CNC lathes in our machine park · Machine list

Technology 02

CNC milling

Milling, drilling and threading of prismatic parts on vertical and horizontal machining centres, including 5-axis machining.

  • Chiron FZ16 / FZ22 high-speed machining centres
  • Bridgeport VMC 1000 5-axis machining
  • OKK, KIWA, Akira-Seiki and Bridgeport Interact machines
  • Secondary milling and drilling of turned parts

10 CNC machining centres · Machine list

Technology 03

Casting machining

Finish machining of aluminium and brass castings in series.

  • Fixturing of castings, machining of datum faces and fits
  • Milling, drilling and threading in one or more set-ups
  • In-process and final inspection in our own measuring room

Service 04

Series production and full supply

Medium and large series production in 3 shifts. We cover the entire process for our partners, from raw material sourcing to delivery of the finished product.

  • Raw material sourcing and handling
  • CNC saws for material cutting (Bomar, Forte)
  • Production management and SAP-based traceability
  • Packaging and delivery to customer requirements
  • One-off parts and small batches on conventional and NC machines as well
