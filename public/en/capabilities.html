Technology 01

CNC turning

Complete machining of rotational parts from bar stock on CNC automatic and Swiss-type lathes.

  • INDEX GS30 twin-turret sub-spindle automatic; STAR SA, SB, SR and SR 32 J automatic lathes
  • Twin-turret lathes with C-axis and sub-spindle: Mori Seiki ZL, Nakamura TW
  • Small, complex precision parts on Swiss-type lathes
  • Takisawa NEX108 / NEX108Y (Y-axis) and EX910 CNC lathes, EEN NC centre lathes

24 CNC lathes + 5 NC centre lathes · Machine list

    Technology 02

    CNC milling

    Milling, drilling and threading of prismatic parts on vertical machining centres with Fanuc, Heidenhain and Mitsubishi controls.

    • Akira Seiki SV, SR, V4 and RMV vertical machining centres
    • Bridgeport Interact VMC1000 3D with Heidenhain control
    • Mori Seiki SV403 and OKK MCV-410 with Fanuc control
    • Secondary milling and drilling of turned parts

    11 CNC machining centres · Machine list

    Technology 03

    Casting machining

    Finish machining of aluminium and brass castings in series.

    • Fixturing of castings, machining of datum faces and fits
    • Milling, drilling and threading in one or more set-ups
    • In-process and final inspection in our own measuring room

    Technology 04

    General metal cutting

    Metalworking on conventional machines; custom, small but geometrically complex parts.

    • Heat-treated and ground machine parts
    • Manufacture of tools and machine assemblies
    • Prototypes and small batches

    Service 05

    Series production and full supply

    Medium and large series production in 3 shifts. We cover the entire process for our partners, from raw material sourcing to delivery of the finished product.

    • Raw material sourcing and handling
    • Band saws for material cutting (Bomar, Forte)
    • Production management and SAP-based traceability
    • Packaging and delivery to customer requirements
    • One-off parts and small batches on conventional and NC machines as well
