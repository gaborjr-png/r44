About us

29 years in metal cutting

Steel Riders Kft. was founded in March 1997 as a family business in Verpelét, Hungary. Our core activity is metal cutting: one-off workpieces and series production.

The company

We started with 3 people in a 200 m² workshop. Today we are a stable company of 50 people: we have been in the supplier market for more than 20 years and have sold 70,000,000 parts.

As the machine park was expanded and modernised, new production halls were built. We are still based in Verpelét: on a 27,000 m² company-owned industrial site with an 800 m² production plant and a modern 1,200 m² production hall opened in 2016 with EU funding.

Our production is built on 38 CNC lathes and 7 machining centres, complemented by numerous other machine tools. Production runs in 3 shifts. We are customer-centric: we strive to deliver the highest possible quality, on time and meeting customer requirements in every respect.

To meet the growing requirements of the domestic and international markets, we operate ISO 9001 quality management and ISO 14001 environmental management systems, our own measuring room and an integrated SAP ERP system.

History

Milestones

  1. 1997

    Foundation

    We start as a family business in Verpelét with 3 people in a 200 m² workshop. Core profile: metal cutting, one-off parts and series production.

  2. Growth

    Own site and production plant

    As the machine park grows, production moves to our own 27,000 m² industrial site and an 800 m² plant.

  3. 2016

    New 1,200 m² production hall

    A modern production hall is completed with EU funding, expanding our series production capacity.

  4. 2020

    Supplier development – Irinyi Plan

    In a project aimed at developing our supplier activities, we introduce new STAR SB-20R type G CNC automatic lathes and an efficiency-boosting production management system, together with staff training.

  5. 2021

    HC-02.2 helicopter

    On 8 June the two-seat HC-02.2 helicopter, built together with Hungaro-Copter Kft., receives the permits required for test flights.

  6. 2026

    Today

    38 CNC lathes, 7 machining centres, 50 employees, 3 shifts and 70,000,000 parts sold.
